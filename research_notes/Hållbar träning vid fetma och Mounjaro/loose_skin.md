# Loose/excess skin after major weight loss: what drives it and what can realistically be influenced

**Context for the reader:** user is a 51-year-old woman, ~160 cm, ~140 kg, so BMI ≈ 54.7. She is starting tirzepatide (Mounjaro) plus home training. Her top worry is hanging skin on the upper arms and inner thighs.

**Method caveat:** these notes rest on web-search snippets and abstracts, not full texts. This session's network blocked full-text fetches from pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov, pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov, gupea.ub.gu.se, gu.se, vardgivare.skane.se and emjreviews.com. Figures marked "(abstract/snippet)" should be checked against the full paper before they appear verbatim in the app.

**Evidence grades used below:**
- **A**: RCT or meta-analysis
- **B**: prospective or large observational cohort
- **C**: small, cross-sectional or survey study
- **D**: expert opinion, mechanism only or marketing claim

## 1. Prevalence of excess skin after massive weight loss, and which regions are affected

### Takeaway
Excess skin is close to the norm after massive weight loss, not the exception. In Swedish gastric-bypass cohorts, 84–96% report it. The abdomen is the most common site, and the upper arms and inner thighs come next. Women report more affected regions than men, specifically the upper arms and medial thighs. So the user's feared regions are exactly the typical female pattern.

### Cited Findings
- **Biörserud et al. 2011, *Obesity Surgery*** (Gothenburg). Questionnaire study of 112 laparoscopic gastric-bypass patients. **84% (94/112) reported problems with surplus skin.** Surplus skin was most common on the **abdomen, upper arms and inner thighs**. Women reported more problems and more affected body parts, specifically the upper arms, medial thigh and lateral back. Grade C/B. — [Springer Medizin abstract](https://www.springermedizin.de/patients-experience-of-surplus-skin-after-laparoscopic-gastric-b/9035946)
- **Kitzinger et al. 2012, *Obesity Surgery*** ("After massive weight loss: patients' expectations of body contouring surgery"). **96% developed surplus skin.** It caused intertriginous dermatitis and itching, and problems with physical activity and with finding clothes that fit. Grade C. — [QxMD abstract](https://read.qxmd.com/read/22101887/after-massive-weight-loss-patients-expectations-of-body-contouring-surgery)
- **Ockell / Biörserud line (Gothenburg).**
  - Prospective measurement of 147 patients before and 18 months after surgery.
  - **Each centimetre of pre-operative ptosis (sagging) gave 1.37× higher odds of high post-operative discomfort from upper-arm skin, and 1.31× for thighs.** So the amount of sag already present before weight loss predicts later bother.
  - Thigh ptosis was the only objective measure that increased significantly after surgery.
  - In a normal-population comparison group, 78% reported no excess skin.
  - Grade B. — [Ockell thesis, Univ. of Gothenburg 2023 (GUPEA)](https://gupea.ub.gu.se/handle/2077/75212); [earlier thesis 2015](https://gupea.ub.gu.se/handle/2077/38370) (snippet-level; full text not accessible)
- **Adolescents after gastric bypass** (Swedish AMOS cohort, 47 adolescents, *Plast Reconstr Surg* 2014). The most common locations of excess skin on measurement were the **upper arms and thighs**. So young skin does not protect against it either. Grade B/C. — [PubMed 25357024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25357024/); [Lund Univ. record](https://lup.lub.lu.se/record/4787476)
- **Giordano et al. 2013** (360 post-bariatric patients). The study documents physical discomfort from redundant skin. Grade C. — [PubMed 23578737](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23578737/)
- **Reviews.** The abdomen is the most frequently affected region, followed by breasts, arms, thighs and buttocks. Grade D (review). — [PMC review 7423629](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7423629)
- **Function, not just looks.** Excess skin causes rashes and itching, makes exercise harder and makes clothes hard to fit ([Kitzinger 2012](https://read.qxmd.com/read/22101887/after-massive-weight-loss-patients-expectations-of-body-contouring-surgery)).
  - In 26 women, avoidance of physical activity had "less to do with the magnitude of ES itself and more with psychosocial inconveniences." — [PubMed 23620304](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23620304/)
  - Qualitative interviews (n = 15): "the body was still a hindrance for physical activity but now because of excess skin." — [search-indexed abstract, *J Plast Surg Hand Surg* 2020](https://informahealthcare.com/doi/abs/10.1080/2000656X.2020.1777144)
- **GLP-1 era.**
  - Survey of 1,226 people on pharmacological weight management (telemedicine platform): **>60% reported concerns** such as skin laxity, facial volume loss, hair shedding or reduced strength. Concerns rose with the amount of weight lost.
  - Concerns were more frequent with GLP-1 RAs, but GLP-1 users also lost more weight, so drug and weight-loss effects cannot be separated.
  - Grade C (anonymous voluntary survey; snippet only). — [EMJ/AMJ news summary](https://www.emjreviews.com/dermatology/news/skin-hair-and-muscle-changes-reported-during-glp-1-weight-loss)

### Inferences
- Bariatric cohorts (typically 30–40% total weight loss) are the best available proxy. Mean weight loss with tirzepatide is ~20–22% at 72 weeks (see section 4), so a typical Mounjaro user loses less than a bariatric patient. That should mean somewhat less excess skin on average, but not none.
- The user's starting BMI is ~55. Even a ~20% loss (≈28 kg → ~112 kg, BMI ≈ 44) is a large absolute volume change. A good responder losing 30%+ (≈42 kg) would be squarely in "massive weight loss" territory, conventionally ≥50% of excess weight or a large BMI drop.
- For a woman, arms and inner thighs are among the most likely sites. Honest guidance must say so plainly rather than imply that she can avoid it.

### Gaps
- No prospective study was found that measures excess-skin prevalence specifically after tirzepatide or semaglutide weight loss. All GLP-1 data are surveys or opinion.
- Region-specific prevalence percentages for women (e.g. % with upper-arm excess) are in the Biörserud and Ockell full texts, which could not be accessed.

## 2. Determinants: amount lost, starting BMI, age, rate of loss, genetics, sun, smoking, skin biology

### Takeaway
- **Best-supported determinants:**
  - female sex
  - amount of weight / BMI lost
  - the highest BMI ever reached
  - how much sagging is already present before the loss
- **Age:** relates more to how *bothersome* the skin is than to how much there is (one study). It is mechanistically plausible but not consistently shown.
- **Rate of weight loss:** the idea that slower loss protects is an **assumption with essentially no direct human evidence**.
- **Smoking and sun:** damage elastic tissue (mechanistic and histological evidence), but nobody has shown that they change excess-skin outcomes after weight loss.

### Cited Findings
- **Amount of loss and BMI.**
  - Giordano 2013 (n = 360): on multivariate analysis, excess skin was associated with **female gender, amount of weight loss and ΔBMI**. Grade C/B. — [PubMed 23578737](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23578737/)
  - Baillot et al. 2023, *Obesity Surgery* (mixed-methods, n = 124, 92% women, mean age 46.5). Total excess-skin quantity correlated with **maximal BMI reached before surgery (r = 0.48)** and **current BMI (r = 0.35)**. Grade C. — [PubMed 37389805](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37389805/); [medRxiv full text](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.04.26.23289160.full.pdf)
- **Age.**
  - In Baillot 2023, age was **not** associated with excess-skin quantity. It was associated with **greater inconvenience**: inconvenience was explained by social physique anxiety plus age (R² = 0.50).
  - The paper's background notes that sex, not age, relates to excess-skin quantity, and that evidence on other factors is mixed.
  - Grade C. — [PubMed 37389805](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37389805/)
- **Pre-existing sag.**
  - Each cm of pre-operative ptosis raised the odds of high post-op discomfort for arms (1.37×) and thighs (1.31×) (Ockell/Biörserud). Grade B. — [GUPEA thesis](https://gupea.ub.gu.se/handle/2077/75212)
  - In Teen-LABS, higher pre-operative BMI was associated with a more severe abdominal pannus. — [as summarised in Baillot 2023 background](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.04.26.23289160.full.pdf)
- **Rate of weight loss.**
  - A clinician quoted by WW states "there's just no strong research comparing GLP-1, bariatric surgery, and gradual weight loss — and their respective amount of skin laxity." Grade D. — [WeightWatchers blog](https://www.weightwatchers.com/us/blog/loose-skin-after-weight-loss)
  - The 1,226-person pharmacotherapy survey did **not** capture rate of loss, so it cannot say anything about it. — [EMJ summary](https://www.emjreviews.com/dermatology/news/skin-hair-and-muscle-changes-reported-during-glp-1-weight-loss)
  - A JDD review states that rapid weight loss affects collagen, elastic fibres, adipose tissue and muscle. It cautions that the changes "reflect a broader physiological response to rapid loss" rather than the drug itself. Grade D (narrative review). — [J Drugs Dermatol 2026](https://jddonline.com/articles/weight-loss-its-impact-on-soft-tissue-S1545961626P9532X/)
  - Duke study, >500 abdominal-contouring patients. Those who combined bariatric surgery and GLP-1 lost the most (~29%) and fastest, and had the highest **surgical complication** rates. It measured complications, not laxity. Grade C. — [Duke Surgery news](https://surgery.duke.edu/news/age-glp-1s-plastic-surgeons-face-new-reality)
  - **Red flag:** GLP-1 vendor sites (e.g. FormBlends) cite "3.2× higher excess skin with rapid loss" from a "Coon 2019 / Stinkens 2017" study. These figures could not be verified and should be treated as unreliable marketing. — [FormBlends page, for reference only](https://formblends.com/articles/glp1-hub/how-to-avoid-loose-skin-after-weight-loss)
- **Skin biology after massive weight loss.**
  - **Collagen:** dermal collagen is thinner and disorganised in massive-weight-loss skin. Collagen was significantly thinner in papillary and reticular dermis on image analysis, and there are "thin, misaligned, loosely arranged fibers" replacing organised thick fibres. Grade C (small histology series). — [Eplasty image-analyser study](https://www.hmpgloballearningnetwork.com/site/eplasty/image-analyzer-study-skin-patients-morbid-obesity-and-massive-weight-loss)
  - **Elastic fibres:** findings conflict, with some studies showing damage or reduction and one showing increased density.
  - **Surgical vs. non-surgical loss:** a 2024 *Obesity Surgery* comparison found **reduced elastic-fibre content in abdominal skin after surgical massive weight loss compared with non-surgical**. Grade C. — [Obes Surg 2024 (Springer PDF)](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s11695-024-07066-y.pdf); [QxMD 38277086](https://read.qxmd.com/read/38277086/comparison-of-histological-skin-changes-after-massive-weight-loss-in-post-bariatric-and-non-bariatric-patients)
- **Smoking.**
  - More numerous, thickened, fragmented elastic fibres in non-sun-exposed skin of smokers. The authors read this as degradation resembling solar elastosis, called smoking "an independent risk factor," and found a dose–response with cumulative tobacco. — [Br J Dermatol 2007 via QxMD](https://www.qxmd.com/r/17199572)
  - Chronic smoking remodels the elastic network and **increases dermal and epidermal stiffness**. — [J Pathol 2020 (Liverpool repository)](https://livrepository.liverpool.ac.uk/3089772/)
  - **Contradicted by** a 2002 study of 98 men that found no difference in elastic fibres or elasticity. — [Acta Derm Venereol 2002](https://medicaljournalssweden.se/actadv/article/view/13662)
  - Overall grade C (histology/mechanics); there are no outcome data after weight loss.

### Inferences
- **The main determinant is not modifiable once you have obesity.** That determinant is how much and for how long the skin has been stretched (maximum BMI, existing sag), plus sex. The largest single variable she controls is how much weight she loses. That creates a paradox: more successful loss means more visible excess skin. The app must frame excess skin as a **side effect of success**, not a failure.
- **The user's specific risk profile:**
  - female
  - very high maximum BMI (~55)
  - age 51, so skin collagen is declining with age, though age was not a strong quantity predictor in Baillot
  - probably long-standing obesity
  - Prior expectation: high likelihood of some arm and inner-thigh laxity if she loses a large amount.
- **"Lose slowly to avoid loose skin" should not be presented as evidence-based.** Slower loss is reasonable for other reasons, such as muscle preservation, GI tolerability, gallstones and sustainability. The skin claim is a hypothesis. A plausible confounder: people who lose fast usually lose *more*.
- **Duration of obesity and genetics:** frequently asserted. No quantified human data were found in this session.

### Gaps
- No controlled study compares skin outcomes at different rates of loss with the same total loss.
- No quantified data on duration of obesity, genetics, Fitzpatrick skin type or striae as predictors of excess skin after massive weight loss.
- Ockell/Biörserud full multivariable models (age, BMI, weight-loss predictors) were not accessible.
- Striae (stretch marks): not researched in depth in this session. The Skåne referral instruction notes that striae alone are **not** an indication for public abdominoplasty (see section 5).

## 3. Modifiable factors and their honest effect sizes

### Takeaway
- **Nothing has been shown to prevent excess skin after massive weight loss.**
- **Resistance training** cannot spot-reduce fat or tighten skin. It *can* add some muscle under the skin, preserve lean mass during drug-driven loss, and improve strength and function. The muscle volume gained in the arms and inner thighs is small relative to the fat volume lost.
- **Oral collagen** produces a small, contested improvement in measured skin elasticity in ageing skin. There are no data in massive weight loss, and the benefit disappears in non-industry-funded and high-quality trials.
- **Not smoking and sun protection** are sensible ways to protect skin quality. Their effect on post-loss laxity is unquantified.

### Cited Findings
- **Spot reduction does not occur.**
  - Kostek et al. 2007, *Med Sci Sports Exerc*: 104 subjects, 12 weeks of resistance training of **one arm**.
  - MRI showed "a generalized subcutaneous fat loss independent of gender, supporting the notion that spot reduction does not occur."
  - Skinfolds had suggested a local drop in men only, which the authors attributed to measurement artefact.
  - Grade A/B (controlled within-subject trial). — [PubMed 17596787](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17596787/)
- **Regional muscle hypertrophy is real but modest.**
  - Triceps trained with arms overhead (long muscle length) grew **+19.9% vs +13.9%** with arms at the sides (d = 0.54). Duration was not given in the snippet, and this is a preprint. Grade B/C. — [SportRxiv preprint](https://sportrxiv.org/index.php/server/preprint/download/486/version/623/1038/971)
  - In trained women, exercise resistance profile (e.g. preacher vs. incline curl) determined which **region** of the biceps grew. — [PubMed 37559762](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37559762/)
  - Leg-press training (12 weeks, 2×/week) reportedly induced hypertrophy of the gluteus maximus and **adductor magnus**. The percentage was not available. — (search snippet only, source not verified, see Gaps)
- **Lean-mass preservation on tirzepatide.**
  - SURMOUNT-1 DXA substudy (n = 160), 72 weeks: weight −21.3%, **fat mass −33.9%, lean mass −10.9%**.
  - About **75% of the weight lost was fat and 25% lean**, the same split as placebo. The lean share of body composition rose from 51.0% to 58.1%.
  - Grade A (RCT substudy, post hoc, Lilly-funded). — [Diabetes Obes Metab 2025, PMC11965027](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11965027/)
- **Exercise to prevent excess skin:** **no RCT was found** that tests exercise to prevent excess skin after massive weight loss. Biörserud's registered study on excess skin and activity (NCT01527851) is observational. — [search summary / trial listing](https://decentrialz.com/clinical-trials/NCT01527851/excess-skin-and-physical-activity)
- **Oral collagen.**
  - Pu et al. 2023, *Nutrients*: meta-analysis of 26 RCTs (n = 1,721). Hydrolysed collagen improved skin **hydration and elasticity** vs placebo. The authors noted several biases in the included RCTs. Grade A (low-moderate certainty). — [MDPI Nutrients 15(9):2080](https://www.mdpi.com/article/10.3390/nu15092080)
  - *American Journal of Medicine* 2025 meta-analysis (23 RCTs, n = 1,474):
    - Pooled data showed benefit.
    - **Studies without industry funding showed no effect on any endpoint, and high-quality studies showed no significant effect.**
    - Industry bodies dispute the funding classification.
    - Grade A (but contested). — [NutraIngredients report](https://nutraingredients.com/Article/2025/08/26/industry-reacts-to-meta-analysis-concluding-collagen-supplements-show-no-proven-benefit-for-skin-aging)
  - A 2026 updated meta-analysis reportedly found SMD ≈ 0.62 for elasticity, with wrinkles and dermal density "unproven." — [Dermatology Times summary](https://www.dermatologytimes.com/view/oral-collagen-improves-skin-hydration-and-elasticity-but-benefits-for-wrinkles-remain-unproven)
  - None of these trials were in post-weight-loss skin or measured skin *excess* or *laxity of redundant folds*. They measured cutometer elasticity of facial or forearm skin in mostly middle-aged women.
- **Smoking:** see section 2. Smoking remodels and stiffens dermis ([J Pathol 2020](https://livrepository.liverpool.ac.uk/3089772/)). Being smoke-free is also a **hard requirement for public body-contouring surgery** in Sweden ([Karolinska](https://www.karolinska.se/vard/tema/tema-akut-och-reparativ-medicin/plastikkirurgi-och-kakkirurgi/plastikkirurgi-och-kakkirurgi/plastikkirurgi-och-kakkirurgi/bukplastik/); [Region Skåne guideline](https://vardgivare.skane.se:443/siteassets/1.-vardriktlinjer/regionala-riktlinjer---fillistning/plastikkirurgi-efter-kraftig-viktnedgang---riktlinje.pdf)).
- **Non-surgical options:** "No single non-surgical procedure corrects large tissue excess" (Swedish patient-information site). Grade D. — [Vården.se](https://www.varden.se/halsa/overflodig-hud)

### Inferences
- **Overall effect-size verdict for the app** (graded by the author of these notes):

| Factor | Effect on post-loss excess skin | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Total amount lost / max BMI / sex / pre-existing sag | Large, mostly fixed | B |
| Resistance training | No spot fat loss or skin tightening shown. Modest regional muscle gain (~10–20% muscle thickness over months in trials). Preserves lean mass and function. | A (no spot reduction); B (hypertrophy); none for skin |
| Adequate protein | Supports lean-mass retention. No skin data in this context. | Indirect; not researched here |
| Oral collagen | Small cutometer elasticity gain in ageing skin, possibly bias-driven. Unknown for hanging skin. | A but contested |
| Vitamin C, hydration, moisturisers, retinoids | No outcome data on excess skin after weight loss found | None/D |
| Not smoking, sun protection | Protect elastic tissue in general. Required for surgery eligibility. | C (mechanistic) |
| Slower rate of loss | Unproven for skin | None |

- **Why muscle cannot "fill" the skin much in her case:**
  - The arm skin envelope stretched by a large subcutaneous fat volume.
  - In women, upper-arm muscle cross-section is modest, and novice hypertrophy of ~10–20% in thickness over months adds only a small fraction of the lost volume.
  - Training can improve the *look and firmness* of the area under mild laxity. It cannot eliminate a large skin apron ("bat wing").
  - This is reasoning from the data above, not a measured outcome.
- **Training is still the single best thing she can do for her body during Mounjaro.** About 25% of the loss is lean mass (SURMOUNT-1). Resistance training is the main tool to shift that ratio. It also improves strength, function and metabolic health, and is useful preparation for any later surgery. The app should sell training on these grounds, not as a skin cure.
- **Topical retinoids and moisturisers:** may improve surface texture of photo-aged skin. That evidence is from photoageing research, not this context. Do not promise any effect on laxity.

### Gaps
- No RCT tests resistance training, protein, collagen, vitamin C, hydration or topicals for preventing or reducing excess skin after massive weight loss.
- Quantified adductor hypertrophy data, and the exact duration in the triceps overhead study, were not obtained.
- Protein-intake targets and lean-mass data on GLP-1 were not researched in this session (presumably covered by other notes).
- Unverifiable: the GLP-1 vendor-cited "Stinkens 2017 RCT" and "Coon 2019" figures.

## 4. "Ozempic skin" / "Ozempic body": rapid GLP-1 weight loss and skin laxity

### Takeaway
"Ozempic skin/face/body" is a media label for the ordinary consequences of large weight loss (volume loss and laxity). There is **no evidence of a drug-specific skin effect**. The only data are patient and clinician surveys and narrative reviews.

### Cited Findings
- **Patient survey** (n = 1,226): concerns about skin laxity etc. rose with the **amount of weight lost**. The higher rate in GLP-1 users cannot be separated from their greater weight loss. — [EMJ summary](https://www.emjreviews.com/dermatology/news/skin-hair-and-muscle-changes-reported-during-glp-1-weight-loss)
- **Clinician survey** (AAD 2026): clinicians estimated ~52% of GLP-1-treated patients expressed concern about facial appearance. Grade D. — [Modern Aesthetics](https://modernaesthetics.com/news/aad-2026-survey-highlights-facial-concerns-among-glp-1-weight-loss-patients/2486397/)
- **JDD 2026 review:** attributing skin changes exclusively to GLP-1 agonists "might neglect other physiological factors associated with rapid weight loss." — [JDD](https://jddonline.com/articles/weight-loss-its-impact-on-soft-tissue-S1545961626P9532X/)
- **Expected magnitude of loss on tirzepatide:** mean −21.3% body weight at 72 weeks in the SURMOUNT-1 DXA substudy. — [PMC11965027](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11965027/)
- **Combined bariatric surgery plus GLP-1 patients:** the most (~29%) and fastest weight loss, and the highest complication rates at abdominal contouring (Duke). — [Duke Surgery](https://surgery.duke.edu/news/age-glp-1s-plastic-surgeons-face-new-reality)

### Inferences
- For a 140 kg user, a SURMOUNT-like response is ~28–30 kg. A strong response (25–30%+) is ~35–45 kg.
- The pace on tirzepatide (typically fastest in months 3–9) is in the same range as early post-bariatric loss. It is not uniquely fast.
- The app should de-dramatise "Ozempic skin" and frame it as "this is what big weight loss does to skin, whatever the method."

### Gaps
- No prospective objective measurements (e.g. ptosis in cm or laxity scales) of arm or thigh skin before and after GLP-1 therapy were found.

## 5. Psychological impact, framing, and options afterwards, including Swedish public-healthcare criteria

### Takeaway
- **Psychological impact:** excess skin genuinely affects body image, physical activity and quality of life. Bother is driven as much by body-image anxiety as by the amount of skin.
- **Surgery:** body-contouring surgery produces large and durable QoL gains.
- **Public surgery in Sweden** is regionally regulated. Typical requirements:
  - **BMI around ≤30** (varies by region)
  - **weight stable ≥6 months**
  - **smoke-free**
  - a measurable functional skin excess
  - in Skåne, an **original BMI ≥35**

  For this user, reaching BMI ~30 means ~77 kg, a loss of ~63 kg (~45%). That is beyond the average tirzepatide response, so she may not meet public criteria. This must be communicated carefully and verified with her region.

### Cited Findings
- **Bother vs. quantity.**
  - Excess-skin inconvenience was associated with **social physique anxiety and age (R² = 0.50)**, not with measured quantity.
  - Self-reported quantity and inconvenience were associated with body-image concerns.
  - — [Baillot 2023, PubMed 37389805](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37389805/)
- **Activity avoidance** relates more to psychosocial inconvenience than to the size of the excess skin (26 women). — [PubMed 23620304](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23620304/)
- **Benefit of surgery.**
  - Body contouring gives sustained improvement in HRQL and appearance for up to 10 years (*Ann Surg* 2022, international matched cohort). — [UMC Utrecht record](https://researchinformation.umcutrecht.nl/en/publications/body-contouring-surgery-after-bariatric-surgery-improves-long-ter/)
  - Matched comparison (n = 57): the surgical group improved on 10/11 BODY-Q scales. The non-operative group did not improve and worsened on 4 scales. — [search result, *Plast Reconstr Surg* 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36728585/)
  - Danish cohort (n = 1,527): lower QoL scores over time in those who did not get contouring. — [PMC10138039](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10138039/)
  - Polish 12-month BODY-Q follow-up: improved acceptance of body appearance. — [PMC7034793](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7034793/)
- **Swedish criteria.**
  - **Region Skåne guideline**, "Plastikkirurgi efter kraftig viktnedgång":
    - adopted 2023-05-31, **valid until 2026-05-31**, so it may now be expired or replaced and must be checked
    - based on SKL 2008 national medical indications for abdominoplasty and breast reduction
    - requires weight loss and weight stability, ASA ≤2, stopping smoking and alcohol
    - requires an **original BMI ≥35** to count as "kraftig viktnedgång"
    - names hips/thighs and upper arms among the regions
    - — [Region Skåne riktlinje (PDF)](https://vardgivare.skane.se:443/siteassets/1.-vardriktlinjer/regionala-riktlinjer---fillistning/plastikkirurgi-efter-kraftig-viktnedgang---riktlinje.pdf)
  - **Skåne SUS referral instruction:** abdominoplasty requires an apron ("häng") of **≥5 cm**. **Striae alone are not an indication.** — [SUS remissinstruktion (PDF)](https://vardgivare.skane.se:443/siteassets/2.-patientadministration/remisshantering-och-rutiner-for-vantande-patienter/lokala-tillagg---fillistning/plastikkirurgi-sus-remissinstruktion-brost-och-bukreduktioner-samt-gynekomastier.pdf)
  - **Karolinska (Stockholm), public abdominoplasty:** lower-abdominal excess ≥5 cm, weight stable ≥6 months, non-smoker. — [Karolinska bukplastik](https://www.karolinska.se/vard/tema/tema-akut-och-reparativ-medicin/plastikkirurgi-och-kakkirurgi/plastikkirurgi-och-kakkirurgi/plastikkirurgi-och-kakkirurgi/bukplastik/)
  - **National knowledge support (breast reduction, NPO, 2024-02-27):** BMI limit changed to **<30 for all**, from <25 for women under 50 and <27 for women over 50. — [1177 vårdpersonal konsekvensbeskrivning (PDF)](https://vardpersonal.1177.se/globalassets/nkk/nationell/media/dokument/kunskapsstod/bilagor-kliniska-kunskapsstod/konsekvensbeskrivning-for-kunskapsstod-brostreduktion-240227.pdf)
  - **Denmark, for comparison:** weight loss ≥15 BMI units, BMI <30, weight stable ≥6 months. — [sundhed.dk](https://sundhed.dk/sundhedsfaglig/information-til-praksis/midtjylland/almen-praksis/patientbehandling/patientforloeb/forloebsbeskrivelser-icpc/t-endokrinologi-metabolik-ernaering/plastikkirurgi-vaegttab-fedmekirurgi)
  - **Procedures:** brachioplasty (armplastik) removes loose upper-arm skin. Thigh lift (lårplastik) is often combined with liposuction. — [Vården.se](https://www.varden.se/halsa/overflodig-hud)
- **Uptake:** Kitzinger et al. 2012 also published "The prevalence of body contouring surgery after gastric bypass surgery" (*Obes Surg* 22(1):8–12). The numbers were not retrieved. — [listed in reference results](https://read.qxmd.com/read/22101887/after-massive-weight-loss-patients-expectations-of-body-contouring-surgery)

### Inferences
- **Framing principles:**
  - Excess skin is common, expected and a marker of the work done. Its *impact* is strongly shaped by body-image anxiety, which is modifiable (self-compassion, function-focused goals, peer normalisation).
  - Promote function-focused goals: strength, ability to climb stairs, play with kids. Avoid appearance-only goals.
- **Practical implication for the user:**
  - Public contouring in Sweden is typically tied to near-normal BMI (~30) and ≥6 months of weight stability.
  - She would need a very large loss to qualify. A realistic pathway may involve long-term weight maintenance, possibly combined bariatric approaches, or private surgery at her own cost.
  - The app should not imply that surgery is guaranteed or free. It should advise contacting vårdcentral/region early to learn local rules.
- **Weight stability:** surgery requires a stable weight. The app can frame the long maintenance phase as a necessary step, not a waiting room.

### Gaps
- No Västra Götaland (Vårdgivarwebben) or Stockholm (Vårdgivarguiden) regional criteria for **arm or thigh** lifts were retrieved. The Skåne guideline (possibly expired) is the only full regional document identified.
- No 1177 patient page specifically on body contouring after weight loss was found.
- The national SKL 2008 indications document itself was not retrieved.
- Uptake rates (% who want vs. % who get contouring in Sweden) were not obtained.

## 6. Training for the upper arms (triceps, biceps, deltoids) and inner thighs (adductors): how much regional muscle can change

### Takeaway
- Targeted training reliably grows the trained muscle, on the order of ~10–20% in muscle thickness over a training block in trials. Exercise choice (e.g. overhead triceps work, lengthened positions) shifts *where* the muscle grows.
- It does **not** remove fat locally and has not been shown to reduce excess skin.
- For this user, the realistic payoff is firmer, stronger arms and thighs and better lean-mass retention during tirzepatide, not prevention of a skin apron.

### Cited Findings
- **Triceps:** overhead (lengthened) training gave +19.9% vs +13.9% for arms-at-side triceps hypertrophy (d = 0.54). Preprint. — [SportRxiv](https://sportrxiv.org/index.php/server/preprint/download/486/version/623/1038/971)
- **Biceps:** resistance profile changed which region of the biceps grew in trained women. — [PubMed 37559762](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37559762/)
- **No spot fat loss:** one-arm training for 12 weeks (n = 104, MRI) gave generalised, not local, subcutaneous fat loss. — [PubMed 17596787](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17596787/)
- **Regional hypertrophy is possible:** NSCA practitioner review. Grade D. — [NSCA PTQ 9.1.1 (PDF)](https://dxpprod.nsca.com/contentassets/dbfde28fefcd4d438039109fe8f68172/ptq-9.1.1-building-a-balanced-and-symmetrical-physique-is-regional-hypertrophy-possible.pdf)
- **Lean mass under tirzepatide:** ~25% of the weight lost is lean mass without a structured training intervention. — [PMC11965027](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11965027/)

### Inferences
- **Home-friendly exercise selection** (practical reasoning, not trial-tested in this population):
  - **Triceps:** overhead extensions with a band or dumbbell, wall or incline push-ups.
  - **Biceps:** curls through full range.
  - **Deltoids:** lateral raises and overhead press.
  - **Adductors:** side-lying adduction, Copenhagen plank regressions, wide-stance (sumo) squats, adductor squeezes with a ball or pillow. Leg press–type multi-joint movements also load the adductor magnus.
- **Progressive overload** (more reps, load or band tension over weeks) is the driver. Starting volume matters less than consistency over months.
- **Joint load at 140 kg:** an early phase with seated or supported variants is sensible. This is handled in other notes.
- **Muscle gain will be partly masked** during an aggressive deficit. Novice "newbie gains" plus GLP-1 deficit realistically means *maintenance or small gains* of arm and thigh muscle, not large hypertrophy. This is inferred from the lean-mass data, with no direct GLP-1 + training RCT retrieved here.

### Gaps
- Adductor-specific hypertrophy percentages were not retrieved.
- No study of resistance training combined with GLP-1 measuring regional (arm or thigh) muscle or skin outcomes was found.

## App design implications

### Takeaway
- **What the app should do:**
  - Be honest that some loose skin, especially on arms and inner thighs, is likely after a large loss and is a sign of success.
  - Sell strength training on what it really does: muscle, strength, function and preserving lean mass on Mounjaro.
  - Promote not smoking and sun protection as low-cost skin hygiene.
  - Give realistic, region-specific information about surgery later.
- **What the app must not do:**
  - Promise that training, slow weight loss, collagen, creams or hydration will prevent or tighten loose skin.

### Cited Findings
- Prevalence 84–96% after bariatric massive weight loss; arms and inner thighs are typical in women. — [Biörserud 2011](https://www.springermedizin.de/patients-experience-of-surplus-skin-after-laparoscopic-gastric-b/9035946); [Kitzinger 2012](https://read.qxmd.com/read/22101887/after-massive-weight-loss-patients-expectations-of-body-contouring-surgery)
- No spot reduction. — [Kostek 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17596787/)
- Collagen benefit is contested and disappears in independent, high-quality trials. — [AJM 2025 via NutraIngredients](https://nutraingredients.com/Article/2025/08/26/industry-reacts-to-meta-analysis-concluding-collagen-supplements-show-no-proven-benefit-for-skin-aging)
- Bother is linked to social physique anxiety. — [Baillot 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37389805/)
- Contouring gives durable QoL gains. Public criteria include stable weight, BMI ~30 and smoke-free status. — [Ann Surg 2022 record](https://researchinformation.umcutrecht.nl/en/publications/body-contouring-surgery-after-bariatric-surgery-improves-long-ter/); [Karolinska](https://www.karolinska.se/vard/tema/tema-akut-och-reparativ-medicin/plastikkirurgi-och-kakkirurgi/plastikkirurgi-och-kakkirurgi/plastikkirurgi-och-kakkirurgi/bukplastik/); [Region Skåne](https://vardgivare.skane.se:443/siteassets/1.-vardriktlinjer/regionala-riktlinjer---fillistning/plastikkirurgi-efter-kraftig-viktnedgang---riktlinje.pdf)

### Inferences

**Emphasise:**
1. **Strength training as a muscle and function tool**, shown with metrics she can feel: reps, load, getting up from the floor, carrying shopping. Regional exercises for triceps, deltoids and adductors are fine to include, but explain that they build muscle under the skin rather than burning fat at that spot.
2. **Protein and lean-mass retention during Mounjaro** (cross-link to the nutrition notes).
3. **Low-cost skin care that is honest:**
   - smoke-free
   - sunscreen and avoiding sunburn
   - moisturiser for comfort and to prevent chafing and rashes in skin folds
   - Frame these as protecting skin quality and comfort, not as prevention.
4. **Skin-fold hygiene and comfort tips** (intertrigo, chafing, compression garments, clothing). Excess skin causes rashes and makes exercise hard.
5. **Body-image support.** Bother is driven by social physique anxiety: use self-compassion prompts, function-focused goals, and normalising stories.
6. **Surgery literacy.** Explain that body contouring exists, has strong QoL evidence, and that public care in Sweden requires stable weight (≥6 months), being smoke-free and a regional BMI threshold (often ~30). Encourage asking vårdcentral early. Do not promise eligibility.

**Must not promise or imply:**
- "Train your arms and thighs and you won't get loose skin" / "tone away bat wings."
- "Lose weight slowly and your skin will keep up." This is unproven, and the main driver is how much is lost.
- "Collagen, vitamin C, water, creams or oils tighten skin." There is no evidence in weight-loss skin; collagen's general evidence is weak and contested.
- Non-surgical devices (RF, ultrasound) as solutions for large excess. Even Swedish patient information says no non-surgical method fixes large excess.
- That public surgery will be available, or on any timeline.
- Do not use "Ozempic skin/body" as a scare term.

**Suggested honest copy themes (Swedish):**
- **Normalise:** "Lös hud efter en stor viktnedgång är vanligt, och det är ett tecken på hur mycket din kropp har förändrats, inte på att du gjort något fel."
- **Honest about what training does:** "Styrketräning bränner inte fett just där du tränar, och den kan inte dra ihop huden. Men den bygger muskler under huden, gör dig starkare och hjälper dig att behålla muskelmassa medan du går ner i vikt."
- **Arms and thighs:** "Övningarna för baksida arm och insida lår ger fastare och starkare muskler. Hur mycket hud som blir över styrs mest av hur mycket du går ner, ditt kön och din hud sedan tidigare, och det kan ingen träning helt ändra på."
- **Speed myth:** "Du kanske har hört att man ska gå ner långsamt för att huden ska hinna med. Det finns inga bra studier som visar det. Det som bevisat spelar roll är hur mycket du går ner totalt."
- **Supplements:** "Kollagentillskott, krämer och mer vatten har inte visats förhindra lös hud efter viktnedgång. Spara pengarna, eller lägg dem på bra mat och träningsutrustning."
- **What you can do:** "Det du faktiskt kan göra för huden: låt bli att röka, skydda dig mot sol och sköt hudvecken så att de inte blir irriterade."
- **Self-compassion:** "Din kropp bär spåren av en lång resa. Fokusera på vad den kan göra: gå längre, lyfta tyngre, orka mer."
- **Surgery, carefully:** "Om huden besvärar dig när vikten har stabiliserats finns det kirurgiska behandlingar, och studier visar att de ofta förbättrar livskvaliteten mycket. Inom offentlig vård i Sverige krävs bland annat stabil vikt i minst 6 månader och rökfrihet, och ofta ett BMI runt 30. Reglerna skiljer sig mellan regioner, så fråga din vårdcentral."
- **Success framing:** "Lös hud är en del av vinsten, inte ett misslyckande."

**Design ideas:**
- Optional "skin check-in" with photos and comfort ratings rather than numbers. It can flag rashes or chafing and suggest seeing a GP.
- A content card "Myter om lös hud" with evidence ratings (stark / svag / saknas).
- Strength progress visualised as rising lines (load, reps) to offset appearance worries.

### Gaps
- Copy should be checked against the current regional rules of the user's region. The Skåne guideline expired in May 2026, and other regions were not retrieved.
- Consider review by a Swedish plastic surgeon or dietitian before shipping medical-adjacent copy.
